## Generation Tips

1. **Style Consistency:**
   - Use "Fallout" or "post-apocalyptic" in every prompt
   - Specify "realistic portrait" for human-like appearance
   - Include "wasteland survivor" for authentic grit

2. **Technical Specs:**
   - Always use --ar 1:1 for square format
   - Generate at 256x256 or higher, then resize
   - For overlays: specify "transparent background"

3. **Character Variety:**
   - Mix ages: young (20s), middle-aged (30s-40s), mature (50s+)
   - Include diverse ethnicities and features
   - Add unique identifying traits (scars, tattoos, hair styles)

4. **Fallout Authenticity:**
   - Weathered/dirty appearance
   - Practical wasteland clothing
   - Expressions showing survival experience
   - Avoid cartoonish or anime styles
