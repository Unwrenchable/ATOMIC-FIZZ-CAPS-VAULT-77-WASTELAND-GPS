**Do not spend money you cannot afford to lose on cryptocurrency assets.**

FIZZ tokens may not be tradeable on any exchange. We make no guarantee that you will be able to sell, swap, or transfer FIZZ tokens. Illiquid tokens may have no practical monetary value.

You are solely responsible for compliance with the laws of your jurisdiction.

## 2. Smart Contract Risk

### 2.1 Code Vulnerabilities

Smart contracts deployed on the Solana blockchain cannot be modified once deployed. Undiscovered vulnerabilities could result in loss of tokens or NFTs. While we take precautions to audit our contracts, no software is completely free of bugs.

### 2.2 Irreversible Transactions

All Solana blockchain transactions are final and irreversible. If you send tokens to a wrong address, there is no mechanism to reverse the transaction. We cannot recover lost tokens.

### 2.3 Network Congestion and Failures

The Solana network may experience congestion, outages, or forks that delay or prevent transactions. We have no control over the Solana network.

## 3. NFT Risk

### 3.1 No Inherent Value

NFTs are digital tokens recorded on the blockchain. They represent in-game items and have no guaranteed monetary value. The value of any NFT is determined entirely by market demand.

### 3.2 Game Dependency

In-game utility of NFTs depends on the continued operation of Atomic Fizz Caps. If the Game is discontinued, NFTs may lose their in-game utility, though you retain the token on-chain.

### 3.3 Transaction Fees Are Non-Refundable

Minting NFTs requires paying Solana network transaction fees. These fees are paid to the Solana network, not to us, and are non-refundable regardless of outcome. See the Mint Gas Disclaimer for details.
