# Atomic Fizz Caps — NFT Mint Gas Disclaimer

**Effective Date: 2025-07-01**

---

## What Are Transaction Fees ("Gas")?

When you mint an NFT item in Atomic Fizz Caps, you are submitting a transaction to the Solana blockchain. Processing that transaction requires a small payment to the network's validators, commonly called a **transaction fee** or "gas."

---

## Who Receives the Fee?

**The Operator does not receive transaction fees.** All fees go directly to Solana validator nodes that process your transaction. We have no control over these fees and receive zero portion of them.

---

## How Much Does It Cost?

Solana transaction fees are generally very low (often less than $0.001 USD per transaction). However:

- Fees are denominated in **SOL** (Solana's native currency)
- The USD equivalent depends on the current SOL market price
- During periods of high network activity, fees may be slightly higher
- Priority fees may be required to guarantee timely processing during congestion

You need SOL in your wallet to pay for transactions. If your wallet does not have enough SOL to cover the fee, the transaction will fail.

---

## Fees Are Non-Refundable

**All transaction fees are final and non-refundable**, regardless of the outcome of the transaction. This includes:

- Failed mints (e.g., if the network rejects the transaction for any reason)
- Mints during network congestion that take longer than expected
- Duplicate transactions submitted in error
- Mints of items that are later changed, deprecated, or removed from the Game

---

## No Guarantee of Mint Success

We cannot guarantee that a mint transaction will succeed. Mint transactions may fail due to:

- Network congestion on Solana
- Insufficient SOL balance in your wallet
- RPC node timeouts
- Smart contract errors
- Account or program state conflicts

If a mint fails, the transaction fee is still consumed by the network. You will not receive the NFT if the transaction fails.

---

## Gas Estimation Before Minting

When possible, the Game's interface will display an estimated transaction fee before you confirm the mint. This estimate may not be exact due to network conditions. The actual fee charged may differ slightly.

---

## Recommendations

Before minting, we recommend:

1. Ensure your wallet has at least **0.1 SOL** available (above the cost of any items you're purchasing), to cover transaction fees
2. Avoid minting during known Solana network congestion periods (check status.solana.com)
3. Double-check the item you are minting before confirming — mints are irreversible
4. Only use official Game interfaces (atomicfizzcaps.xyz) to mint — third-party tools may not behave correctly
